# 🧠 Sequence Models - DeepLearning.AI (Andrew Ng) - Coursera

[The course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/nlp-sequence-models)

Here you will find exercises, notes, and course material completed as part of the **Sequence Models** course, taught by Andrew Ng on Coursera.

Throughout the course, I explored topics including:

* Recurrent neural networks (RNNs), backpropagation through time, and different RNN variants (GRU, LSTM, bidirectional and deep RNNs)
* Sequence generation, language models, and character-level modeling with practical projects (e.g., music generation)
* Word embeddings, Word2Vec, GloVe, and debiasing word vectors for NLP tasks
* Sentiment analysis, named entity recognition, and neural machine translation with attention mechanisms
* Beam search, BLEU score, and error analysis for sequence-to-sequence models
* Attention models for translation and speech-related tasks such as trigger-word detection
* Transformer architectures: self-attention, multi-head attention, and modern sequence modeling with TensorFlow and PyTorch
